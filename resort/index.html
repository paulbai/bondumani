✳ 01 · The village

A small hotel that forgets to be a hotel.

We built Bondumani as a bamboo village first and a hotel second — low structures, a wandering path, trees left where they were, light that behaves like it's on holiday too.

The same gate opens onto Bondumani Art, Sierra Leone's quietly famous painting studio. Stay with us and you sleep surrounded by work that belongs in museums, then wake up a three-minute walk from some of West Africa's finest coastline. This is a rare kind of quiet.

✳ 03 · Bamboo massage

An hour that ends arguments.

Our signature bamboo relaxation treatment is built around warmed, hand-cut bamboo stalks of varying weights — rolled along the back and shoulders in slow, deliberate passes.

The heat softens the muscle, the pressure finds the knots, and the sound of the bamboo — a hollow, woody rhythm — turns out to be the most surprising part of the whole thing.

✳ 04 · Peninsula

Freetown's best-kept coast.

Peninsular Road drops you into a string of beaches locals swear by — River No. 2, Tokeh, Bureh, and Banana Islands just offshore. Bondumani sits walking distance from the quietest stretch.
