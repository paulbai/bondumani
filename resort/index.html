✳ 01 · The village

Not built. Crafted.

Bondumani Bamboo Village opened on Sierra Leone's peninsula in October 2025 — a sanctuary where sustainable architecture meets the sound of the Whale Bay River, and where every wall tells a West-African story.

Rustic A-frame cabins. 24-hour solar power. A restaurant fusing traditional West African flavours with international technique. An outdoor river spa. A yoga clearing among the bamboo. And a curated gallery of contemporary Sierra Leonean art — the same family that runs Bondumani Art in Freetown.

Freetown has many places to stay. There is only one place where the river, the bamboo and the canvas meet.

✳ 04 · The Bondumani difference

True luxury is harmony.

Eco-architecture & artistry

Our village is not just built. It is crafted — bamboo, walnut, canvas and river, assembled by hand.

The original floating lunch

We reimagined the meal. Menus drift beside you as you drift down Whale Bay — West-African flavours, international technique, exclusive house-blend spices.

A sanctuary for the senses

Our river spa has no walls. Yoga and meditation sessions run to the rhythm of the water and the forest around them.

A living culture

We don't host guests. We host culture — intimate gallery exhibitions, proprietary cultural gatherings, heritage made breathable.
