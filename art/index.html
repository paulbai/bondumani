01 · Our Story

A studio born from stubborn belief.

In a city where "be a doctor or a lawyer" is still the loudest advice given to children, two Sierra Leonean painters decided to be neither.

Bondumani Art was founded in Freetown by Sahr Songu Mbriwa — an internationally collected hyperrealistic oil painter — and Aishatu Jalloh, a full-time visual artist with a sharp eye for colour and a softer one for stories. Together they built a place where fabric gets painted like canvas, children learn that brushes are professional tools, and walls in Freetown stop asking to be ignored.

We are a gallery, a studio, a commission house, a classroom, and occasionally — when the bamboo village fills up — a quiet place to sleep.

02 · What we do

Seven obsessions.

Some we've been doing for a decade. Some started when a child asked for a portrait of their grandmother. All of them move.

01 · oil
commission / framed

Portrait painting.

Hyperrealistic oil commissions — from weddings to memorials, sized to your wall and loaded with likeness.

02 · still
graphite / pastel

Still life drawing.

Studies that take Freetown's everyday objects seriously.

03 · face
festival / parade

Face painting.

Bold pigment that stays put and photographs sharp under Freetown sun.

05 · wall
public / private / hotel

Mural painting.

Public walls, private compounds, hotel exteriors — stories sized up for the street.

04 · cloth
wearable / hand

Fabric painting.

Clothing and cloth painted by hand to live in sun, salt and Atlantic air.

06 · kids

Children's art classes.

Our beating heart — training the next generation of West-African painters, one Saturday at a time.

saturdays 6 — 17 ages welcome

06 · The bamboo side

Sleep where the paintings sleep.

Our sister property — Bondumani Bamboo Village — sits on the Whale Bay River, York ↔ Black Johnson. Eco A-frame cabins, a river spa, floating lunches that drift downstream, and a curated on-site gallery hung with the same brushes that paint here in Freetown.
