# Bondumani Bamboo Village

## About the Business

Bondumani Bamboo Village is an eco-friendly adventure retreat and cultural hub located along the Whale Bay River between York and Black Johnson on the Freetown peninsula. It serves as a sanctuary that blends sustainable living with hospitality and art.

**Founded:** October 2025

### What Makes Us Unique

Our village offers a distinct, immersive experience through its rustic A-frame architecture, 24-hour solar power, and direct connection to the river. It stands out by integrating wellness, outdoor recreation, and a deep commitment to showcasing local West African art and culture.

## Services & Offerings

**Stay** — Eco-conscious A-frame huts.

**Dining** — Restaurant serving a blend of traditional West African flavors and international cuisine.

**Activities** — Bamboo rafting, river-based recreational activities, outdoor river spa treatments (massage), yoga, and meditation. All activities can be tailored to preference.

**Art** — On-site gallery featuring curated exhibitions and creative workshops.

## The Bondumani Experience — Our Unique Difference

At Bondumani Bamboo Village, we believe that true luxury is found in the harmony between nature, art, and the soul. While Freetown has many places to stay, there is only one place where the river, the bamboo, and the canvas meet.

We have curated an experience that you will not find anywhere else in Sierra Leone:

### Eco-Architecture & Artistry

Our village is not just built; it is crafted.

### The Original Floating Dining Experience

We have reimagined the meal. Our signature floating lunches allow you to drift along the serene waters of the Whale Bay River, enjoying a menu that fuses deep, traditional West African flavors with an international culinary touch — all prepared with our exclusive house-blended spices.

### A Sanctuary for the Senses

Our River Spa and wellness retreats are defined by the symphony of the forest and the river. By removing the walls of a traditional spa, we allow you to reconnect with yourself, guided by the natural rhythm of the water and the quiet focus of our yoga and meditation sessions.

### A Living Culture

We do not just host guests; we host culture. From our intimate, expertly curated gallery exhibitions to our proprietary cultural gatherings, Bondumani is a living, breathing celebration of our heritage.
