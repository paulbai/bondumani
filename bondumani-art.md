## Brand Overview

**Bondumani Art** is a Sierra Leonean visual arts collective, gallery, and creative studio founded in Freetown, Sierra Leone. Co-founded by **Sahr Songu Mbriwa** and **Aishatu Jalloh**, the business was built on the belief that visual art is a legitimate livelihood and a powerful vehicle for cultural storytelling in Sierra Leone.

**Business Type:** Arts  
**Suggested Tagline:** *Animating the Visual Arts for a Better Sierra Leone*
